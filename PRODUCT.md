# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

Blake's personal portfolio and creative computing environment at moody.mx.

## Users

Visitors explore Blake's work, photography, and music taste. Blake connects his Spotify account; visitors can search for tracks and add them to his queue.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The Music section displays recent listening and Spotify's short-term top track and artist. Spotify's short-term ranking covers approximately four weeks, not a calendar month or exact play counts. Live data requires Blake's authorized Spotify developer app. Client credentials are configured privately; owner authorization is still required to obtain the refresh token.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the established design in DESIGN.md, including monospace density, square borders, glitch headings, both themes, and spinning wireframe objects.
